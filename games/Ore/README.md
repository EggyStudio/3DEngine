# Ore

`games/Ore` is a sample of a voxel landscape under global illumination, 48 by 48 columns of grass,
dirt, stone and emissive ore from a value noise, meshed into a plain mesh whose vertex colors global
illumination reads, walked in the first person by a character controller, under a sun or at night,
where the ore lights the cliffs around it alone. G steps the quality of global illumination, B shows
its window while held, and N turns day to night.

It is built outside the solution from the package `build/pack.sh` makes, as a game of your own would
be, as [BUILDING.md](../../.github/BUILDING.md) says.
