# Slide

`games/Slide` is the puzzle of numbered tiles that merge, played by gestures of a finger or the
mouse, a swipe to slide, a double tap to take a move back, a hold to start again and a pinch to come
nearer, with its tiles' faces drawn into images as it starts and its sounds waves it makes, cuts and
converts.

It is built outside the solution from the package `build/pack.sh` makes, as a game of your own would
be, as [BUILDING.md](../../.github/BUILDING.md) says.
